# Original-slide analytics scope

Verified against original Drive screenshots 14–19 (deck slides 31–36), not just
the condensed presentation. The separate dashboard covers:

- Nightly marketplace revenue and customers, with source-grain restrictions.
- Five pillars: growth, profitability, productivity, inventory and engagement.
- Financial: total e-commerce revenue, YOY revenue growth, gross/net margins,
  revenue/profit per labor hour.
- Production: identified/sent items, listings per day, listings per employee,
  average time to list and unlisted backlog.
- Sales: average/median sale price, sell-through, days to sell, unsold and relisted
  inventory percentages.
- Category: sales, margin, units, sell-through and ASP by category; top ten
  categories by revenue and margin.
- Customer: buyers, repeat-buyer rate, new buyers, satisfaction, NPS and marketplace
  conversion.
- All 15 COO scorecard KPIs, including listings created, donation-to-listing
  interval and sales per employee.
- The 2027 directions: increase net margin, revenue per labor hour and sell-through.

All requested measures must remain visible even when inputs are missing.
Source-local net item sales are not enterprise revenue. Source payout is not
profit. Receipt timestamps are not donation timestamps. Period repeat buyers
are not all-time customer loyalty. Never fabricate labor, COGS, survey, funnel,
prior-year, approved target or cross-marketplace buyer identity.

Existing exact approved-operator authorization remains required for data.
Public screens may show static KPI definitions, not protected values.
No new Clerk tenant, database replacement, paid model call or live vendor access.
Keep the existing Goodwill Reporting app unchanged.
