## Scorecard A: official weighted judging criteria

Published rubric: six dimensions 0–4, weighted to 100. The numbers below are
**criterion weights**, not points earned by the current prototype.

| Official criterion / weight | Timed visible proof (10-minute assumption) | Evidence and current gap | Higher-level evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Working Evidence / 26 | S2–S5 1:25–6:50: actual download, A intake/metric, B parameterization, duplicate and safe edge case | Lane downloads/test evidence exist; continuous integrated accepted recording absent | Level 3 requires full own-example workflow in one take; level 4 adds more than one example including messy/edge input. Cuts/screenshots/narration are not equivalent |
| Partner Problem Fit / 22 | S0 0:00–0:45 and S8 8:40–10:00: Amanda→Debie, less handling, protected close | Partner account sourced; live acquisition and all requested finance/KPIs remain gated | Do not claim fully solves partner problem or fewest possible steps from synthetic slice |
| Fits Their Constraints / 19 | S1 disclosures; S4 deterministic/CSV; S7/S8 policy and tool gates | Authorized synthetic scope/known policy reflected; live approval and paid-tool fit unverified | Highest level includes all key limits and usable tomorrow; not claimed |
| Technical Substance / 15 | S2–S5: verified bytes, state machine, source-local identity, arithmetic, safe stop | Own code versus borrowed fixtures/scaffold identified; lane doubles/integration absence disclosed | Simulated impressive moment is explicitly identified; demonstrate own controls and limits, not stack size |
| Demo Clarity / 11 | First 30 seconds + S1 three layers, A labels, transitions, timed stop | Script complete; actual rehearsal/timing fit not measured | Highest description includes clear first 30 seconds and on-time delivery; human rehearsal needed |
| X-Factor / 7 | S2/S3 approved reusable skill if available; S8 specific handoff/parallel pilot | Differentiator conditional; no confirmed pilot commitment | Judge/partner interest cannot be asserted on their behalf |

Researched theme mapping from M §17/§7:
working evidence → Working Evidence; partner fit → Partner Problem Fit;
constraint fit → Fits Their Constraints; technical substance → Technical Substance;
clarity → Demo Clarity. **Honest disclosure** spans every segment and the
official overclaim flag; it has **no separate weight**. Official X-Factor is a
sixth criterion, not a license to substitute an AI conversation for evidence.
Market size, pricing and polish do not substitute for working evidence.
